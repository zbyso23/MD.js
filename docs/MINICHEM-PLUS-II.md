# MiniChem Plus II — v5.4.5

Offline arrow annotation subset: `->[above]`, `->[above][below]`, plus `<-`, `<->`, `<=>` and unannotated forms. Labels are limited to safe plain text (no TeX), max 48 characters each, max two. Rendering is performed by KaTeX; no chemical calculation or automatic balancing.

Output uses KaTeX extensible arrows; maintain regressions against Plus I and Basic.
