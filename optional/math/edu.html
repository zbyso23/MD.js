Supported syntax and limits

Elements, index numbers, ionic charge, isotopes, hydrates, groups in (…) and […], states (aq), (s), (l), (g), and arrows ->, <-, <->, <=>. Up to two plain-text labels: ->[Pt][300 C]. Not full mhchem. No chemistry validation, atom balancing or chemical calculation.
